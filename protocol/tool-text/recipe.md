Apply a ready-made pattern by name, such as recipe("check-my-work", {problem: ...}), which places a problem, a writing Region and a "Check my work" button in one call. Find Recipes and their params in read_me("index"); read_me(name) explains one.

It is applied as one batch of your own Elements, like apply, and the result includes the ops it expanded to so you can patch or remove any part. To save a new Recipe of your own, use apply with a define op.
